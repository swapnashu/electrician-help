Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: the app does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no advertising or analytics SDK.

1Information the app does not collect

Electrician help has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software.

  • No contacts, photos, files or media access
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No advertising or marketing identifiers
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties

2Sensor data

The spirit-level tool reads your phone’s motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) to show tilt and angle on screen. This reading is processed only on your device, shown only to you, and is never stored or transmitted anywhere.

3Network requests

Two optional features make network requests. Both are strictly voluntary, and every calculator works fully without them.

  • Solar screen (optional): sunrise, sunset and weather for the city you pick are fetched from the Open-Meteo public API. Only the city coordinates are sent — never your device location.
  • Update check: on launch the app asks GitHub for the latest published version number so it can offer you a newer release. No personal data is included in this request.
  • Links: tapping a website, WhatsApp, phone or email link opens that app. What you do there is governed by that app’s own policy.

4Permissions

  • Camera: used only to switch the phone’s torch flashlight on and off. Nothing is captured or saved.
  • Vibrate: used for the level tool’s beep/vibrate alert when the surface is level.
  • Network access: used only by the two optional features described above.

5Children

The app is a professional trade tool and is not directed at children. Since no data is collected from anyone, no children’s data is collected either.

6Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will ship with the app update. Questions about privacy can be sent to satya@electricals.in, or reach us on the WhatsApp group.
